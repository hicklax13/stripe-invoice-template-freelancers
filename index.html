Why use a Stripe invoice template?

Freelancers don't need expensive invoicing software to get paid on time. A clean, professional invoice template — one you control — sends a better client experience and reduces back-and-forth. This free HTML invoice template is built specifically for freelancers who bill through Stripe: it includes line items, tax calculations, discount fields, accepted payment methods, and a one-click PDF export.

Whether you're a designer, developer, writer, consultant, or any independent contractor, this template adapts to your workflow without requiring a subscription.

How to use the template

  • Download the HTML file and open it in Chrome, Edge, Firefox, or Safari.
  • Fill in your company name, address, email, and Stripe payment link in the header section.
  • Add the client's name, address, and invoice details.
  • Enter line items: description, quantity, rate, and any per-line tax or discount.
  • Set the invoice number, issue date, and due date.
  • Click "Download as PDF" to export, or copy the HTML to reuse for future invoices.

Best practices for freelancer invoicing

  • Number every invoice sequentially and keep a running log (Invoice #001, #002, ...).
  • Include payment terms upfront (e.g. "Net 15", "Due on receipt") to set expectations.
  • Break work into clear line items so clients understand what they're paying for.
  • State accepted payment methods and include a Stripe payment link when possible — it reduces friction.
  • Send invoices promptly and follow up politely on overdue invoices.
  • Keep a PDF copy of every sent invoice for your records and taxes.
